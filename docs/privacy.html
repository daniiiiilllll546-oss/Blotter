Privacy

What this site sees.

Blotter has no accounts and no ads, and sells nothing about you. If you accept the cookie, it counts visits so we can see which links bring people here. If you leave your email, it's used for one message. That's all.

Who runs it

Blotter is a non-commercial personal project published by a private individual. For anything about this page or your data, open an issue on the Blotter repository on GitHub, or use the two self-service buttons below.

The cookie

Nothing is recorded until you press Accept cookie. If you do, each page you open records:

  • which Blotter page it was, and the date and time (plus your local hour)
  • the site that linked you here (just its name, like reddit.com) and any campaign tags in the link (utm_source and similar)
  • your country, worked out from your browser's time zone. Your IP address is not stored, and nothing more precise than the country is kept.
  • your browser language, whether you're on a phone, tablet or computer, and how long the page stayed open in front of you

The cookies themselves are first-party, which means they belong to this site only and no ad network or other company can read them:

CookieWhat it holdsHow long
bl_consentYour choice: yes or no12 months

The records are kept in a database run by Supabase in Frankfurt, Germany, and deleted after 12 months. We use them only to see how people find Blotter and what they read. They are never sold or shared.

Choosing No thanks deletes the cookies and every visit recorded under your id.

Your email, if you leave it

The "Real money, later" form stores your email address, the date, the page you used and, if you accepted the cookie, which link brought you here. It is used to send you one email when Blotter's bots start trading real money, and for nothing else. It is never sold or shared, and it stays until you remove it or the project ends.

What your browser loads, and from where

The pages, fonts and scripts are served from GitHub Pages. To show live data, your browser also talks to these services directly. Like any website you visit, each of them sees your IP address and browser details, under its own privacy policy.

What else is stored on your device

Two small settings in your browser's local storage: whether you picked light or dark mode, and whether you've seen the guide or closed the "Start here" note. They never leave your device. Clear your site data to remove them.

Not financial advice

Every result on Blotter is simulated. The bots trade paper money; no real orders are placed and nothing here is a recommendation to buy or sell anything. Blotter is not affiliated with or endorsed by Binance, Anthropic, OpenAI or Codiv.
