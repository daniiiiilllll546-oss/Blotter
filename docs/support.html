Questions

Fair
questions.

Is any of this real money?+

No. Every bot trades a pretend $1,000 wallet. The prices are real and live from Binance, and fees are charged as if the trades were real, but no orders are ever sent to an exchange.

Should I copy the bots' trades?+

Please don't. This is an experiment about which strategies survive, not trading advice. Most bots lose, and that is the point: the cut exists because most of them deserve it.

What does my coffee pay for?+

Running costs: the server, the hourly Claude designer and the OpenJev model calls.
